0How This GA Section Actually Works
GA is 25Q / 50 marks in Tier‑I. Based on SSC CGL 2025 shift-wise analysis, here's exactly where the marks come from.

Static GK — 60–70%

History, Geography, Polity, Economy, Science together account for roughly 15–17 of the 25 GA questions. This is the part you can master permanently — it never changes.

Current Affairs — 30–40%

4–8 questions from the last 6–12 months: appointments, schemes, summits, sports, awards, defence exercises, science/space news. Cover Oct 2025 → exam month.

Realistic Target

15–17 correct out of 25 is a strong, achievable Tier‑I GA score. Static GK should be near-perfect; treat current affairs as the swing factor.

1Ancient India

Indus Valley Civilisation high yield

  • Harappa — Ravi river, Punjab (Pak); first discovered (1921, Daya Ram Sahni); granaries
  • Mohenjo-daro — Indus river, Sindh (1922, R.D. Banerji); "Mound of the Dead"; Great Bath, bronze dancing girl
  • Lothal — Gujarat; dockyard, bead-making
  • Dholavira — Gujarat; water conservation, signboard
  • Kalibangan — Rajasthan; ploughed field, fire altars
  • Script: undeciphered; Town planning: grid pattern, drainage; worshipped Mother Goddess & Pashupati (proto-Shiva)
Trick"HR-MoI-LoG-DoG-KaR" — Harappa-Ravi, Mohenjodaro-Indus, Lothal-Gujarat(dock), Dholavira-Gujarat, Kalibangan-Rajasthan.

Vedic Age

  • Rigveda — oldest text, 10 mandalas, hymns to gods (Agni, Indra)
  • Early Vedic (1500–1000 BCE): pastoral, tribal (Jana), Sabha & Samiti
  • Later Vedic (1000–600 BCE): agriculture, iron use, varna system rigid, Gangetic plains
  • 4 Vedas: Rig, Sama, Yajur, Atharva | Upanishads = philosophical end of Vedas ("Vedanta")

Buddhism & Jainism high yield

  • Buddha (563–483 BCE): born Lumbini, enlightenment at Bodh Gaya, first sermon Sarnath, died Kushinagar — "4 Great Events"
  • 4 Noble Truths, 8-Fold Path, Middle Path; First Council — Rajgriha; language: Pali
  • Mahavira (599–527 BCE): 24th Tirthankara, born Vaishali/Kundagrama; 5 vows incl. Ahimsa; language: Prakrit
  • First Jain council: Pataliputra; Second: Vallabhi
TrickBuddha's life = "Born-Enlightened-Preached-Died" → Lumbini-BodhGaya-Sarnath-Kushinagar (alphabetical-ish order of events, not places).

Mauryan & Gupta Empires

  • Chandragupta Maurya — founded Mauryan empire (321 BCE), guided by Chanakya (Arthashastra)
  • Ashoka — Kalinga War (261 BCE) → embraced Buddhism; Edicts, Sanchi Stupa; symbol: Lion Capital (Sarnath) = national emblem
  • Gupta Age = "Golden Age": Chandragupta I, Samudragupta ("Napoleon of India"), Chandragupta II (Vikramaditya)
  • Gupta era: Aryabhata (zero, astronomy), Kalidasa (Abhijnanashakuntalam), Nalanda University established
2Medieval India

Delhi Sultanate (1206–1526)

  • Slave/Mamluk — Qutb-ud-din Aibak (started Qutub Minar), Iltutmish, Razia Sultana (only woman ruler)
  • Khilji — Alauddin Khilji: market reforms, Chittor conquest
  • Tughlaq — Muhammad bin Tughlaq: capital shift to Devagiri, token currency

Mughal Empire (1526–1857)

  • Babur (1526–30) — founder, 1st Battle of Panipat defeated Ibrahim Lodi
  • Akbar — Din-i-Ilahi, Mansabdari system, Battle of Haldighati (vs Rana Pratap, 1576)
  • Shah Jahan — Taj Mahal, Red Fort, Peacock Throne
  • Aurangzeb — last powerful Mughal; reimposed Jizya; empire declined after
TrickMughal order: "Be Humble, Akbar Just Serves Aurangzeb" — Babur, Humayun, Akbar, Jahangir, Shah Jahan, Aurangzeb.

1Physical Geography — India

Mountain Ranges

  • Himalayas: Himadri (Greater, highest, K2/Everest belt), Himachal (Middle, hill stations), Shiwalik (Outer, foothills)
  • Peninsular: Aravalli (oldest fold mountains, Rajasthan), Western Ghats (Sahyadri — continuous, higher), Eastern Ghats (discontinuous, lower)
  • Highest peak in India: Kanchenjunga (Sikkim) — highest fully within India

Rivers high yield

  • Himalayan (perennial): Ganga (Gangotri), Yamuna (Yamunotri), Brahmaputra (Tibet, enters via Arunachal), Indus (Tibet)
  • Peninsular (rain-fed): Godavari ("Dakshin Ganga", longest peninsular), Krishna, Kaveri, Narmada & Tapi (flow West, rift valleys — exceptions)
  • Ganga tributaries: Yamuna, Gomti, Ghaghra, Son, Chambal, Kosi (Kosi = "Sorrow of Bihar", floods)
  • Longest river in India: Ganga (~2525 km)
Trick"NaTa go West" — Narmada & Tapi are the only major rivers flowing West into Arabian Sea via rift valleys; everything else (Ganga, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri, Mahanadi) flows East into Bay of Bengal.

Monsoon & Climate high yield

  • SW Monsoon (Jun–Sep): main rainy season; enters via Kerala (~1 June); 2 branches — Arabian Sea & Bay of Bengal
  • Mawsynram & Cherrapunji (Meghalaya) — highest rainfall in India/world
  • NE Monsoon (Oct–Dec): "retreating monsoon"; brings rain to Tamil Nadu coast
  • Köppen classification used for India's climate types

Soil Types

  • Alluvial — most fertile, Indo-Gangetic plain, wheat/rice/sugarcane
  • Black (Regur) — Deccan trap, cotton-growing (Maharashtra, MP, Gujarat)
  • Red — southern plateau, iron oxide gives colour
  • Laterite — heavy rainfall areas, tea/coffee/cashew (Kerala, Karnataka)
  • Arid/Desert — Rajasthan; Mountain/Forest soil — Himalayan region
2World Geography

Must-know facts

  • Largest continent: Asia | Smallest: Australia | Largest ocean: Pacific | Longest river: Nile
  • Largest desert (hot): Sahara | Largest cold desert: Antarctica
  • Tropic of Cancer passes through 8 Indian states: Gujarat, Rajasthan, MP, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, WB, Tripura, Mizoram
  • Strait of Malacca — Indonesia/Malaysia; Palk Strait — India/Sri Lanka; Gibraltar — Spain/Morocco
  • International Date Line — 180° longitude, passes through Pacific

India's neighbours & borders

  • Land borders 7 countries: Pakistan, China, Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh, Myanmar, Afghanistan
  • Longest land border with Bangladesh; longest coastline state: Gujarat
  • Radcliffe Line — India-Pakistan; McMahon Line — India-China; Durand Line — Pakistan-Afghanistan
  • India's southernmost point: Indira Point (Great Nicobar) | Northernmost: Indira Col

1Constitution Basics highest-yield subject

Framework

  • Adopted: 26 Nov 1949 (Constitution Day); Enforced: 26 Jan 1950 (Republic Day)
  • Longest written constitution in the world; originally 395 Articles, 22 Parts, 8 Schedules (now 12 Schedules, ~470+ Articles after amendments)
  • Drafting Committee Chairman: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar; Constituent Assembly President: Dr. Rajendra Prasad
  • Borrowed: Fundamental Rights (USA), Directive Principles (Ireland), Parliamentary system (UK), Federal system (Canada)

Preamble

  • Words: Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, Democratic, Republic + Justice, Liberty, Equality, Fraternity
  • "Socialist" & "Secular" added by 42nd Amendment (1976) — "Mini Constitution"
  • Preamble is NOT enforceable in court but reflects the Constitution's spirit (Kesavananda Bharati case, 1973)

3Union & State Government

Parliament

  • Bicameral: Lok Sabha (Lower, max 552, direct election, 5-yr term) + Rajya Sabha (Upper, max 250, indirect, permanent house — 1/3 retire every 2 yrs)
  • Money Bill — only introduced in Lok Sabha (Art 110)
  • President's assent needed for a Bill to become law (Art 111)
  • Speaker of Lok Sabha presides; Rajya Sabha chaired by Vice-President (ex-officio Chairman)

Executive

  • President — Art 52; elected by Electoral College (MPs + MLAs), 5-yr term (Art 56); nominal head
  • PM — real executive head, appointed by President (Art 75), leader of majority in Lok Sabha
  • Governor — head of state, appointed by President (Art 155); CM appointed by Governor (Art 164)
  • Council of Ministers aids & advises President (Art 74) — collectively responsible to Lok Sabha
4Judiciary & Constitutional Bodies

Judiciary

  • Supreme Court — Art 124; apex court, CJI + judges; Art 32 (writs: Habeas Corpus, Mandamus, Prohibition, Certiorari, Quo-Warranto)
  • High Courts — Art 214, one per state (some shared); Art 226 also allows writs
  • Judicial Review — power to examine constitutionality of laws

Constitutional Bodies

  • Election Commission — Art 324, conducts elections (CEC + 2 ECs)
  • CAG — Art 148, audits govt accounts, "guardian of public purse"
  • UPSC — Art 315, recruits civil servants; Finance Commission — Art 280, every 5 yrs (16th FC currently)
  • Attorney General — Art 76, highest law officer of Union

1Core Economic Terms high yield

Basic definitions

  • GDP — total value of goods & services produced within a country's borders
  • GNP — GDP + net income from abroad
  • Inflation — sustained rise in general price level; measured by WPI (wholesale) & CPI (retail — used for inflation targeting)
  • Fiscal Deficit — total expenditure minus total receipts (excluding borrowings)
  • Repo Rate — rate at which RBI lends to commercial banks (key monetary policy tool)
  • Reverse Repo — rate at which RBI borrows from banks
TrickRepo ↑ Rate = RBI tightening (control inflation, reduce money supply); Repo ↓ = RBI easing (boost growth).

Five-Year Plans (for static GK)

  • 1st Plan (1951–56) — agriculture focus, based on Harrod-Domar model
  • 2nd Plan (1956–61) — Mahalanobis model, heavy industries
  • NITI Aayog replaced Planning Commission (2015) — no more Five-Year Plans; 15-year vision, 7-year strategy, 3-year action plan
  • 12th (last) Plan: 2012–2017

Banking & Institutions

  • RBI — est. 1935, central bank, HQ Mumbai; Governor: Sanjay Malhotra (26th Governor)
  • SEBI — regulates securities market; Chairman: Tuhin Kanta Pandey
  • NABARD — agriculture & rural development bank
  • NPA — Non-Performing Asset (loan not repaid for 90+ days)
  • SLR / CRR — Statutory Liquidity Ratio / Cash Reserve Ratio, tools to control liquidity

Tax System

  • Direct tax — paid directly (Income Tax, Corporate Tax); Indirect tax — passed on (GST, Customs)
  • GST — "One Nation One Tax", launched 1 July 2017; subsumed VAT, excise, service tax
  • GST Council — Art 279A, chaired by Union Finance Minister
  • New Income-tax Act 2025 effective from 1 April 2026

1Physics — Quick Facts high yield

Units & Measurement

  • SI unit of Force — Newton | Work/Energy — Joule | Power — Watt | Pressure — Pascal
  • SI unit of temperature — Kelvin; current — Ampere; frequency — Hertz
  • Speed of light in vacuum — 3×10⁸ m/s (fastest)
  • Sound needs a medium; travels fastest in solids, slowest in gases

Laws & Concepts

  • Newton's 3 Laws of Motion (Inertia, F=ma, Action-Reaction)
  • Archimedes' Principle — buoyant force = weight of displaced fluid
  • Pascal's Law — pressure applied to fluid transmitted equally (hydraulic brakes/lifts)
  • Doppler Effect — apparent change in frequency due to relative motion
  • Convex lens — used in microscope/telescope (magnification); Concave — used to correct myopia
2Chemistry — Quick Facts

Everyday chemistry

  • pH scale: 0–14; <7 acidic, 7 neutral, >7 basic; pH of pure water = 7
  • Baking soda — Sodium bicarbonate (NaHCO₃); Common salt — Sodium chloride (NaCl)
  • Hardest natural substance — Diamond (form of carbon)
  • Rusting — oxidation of iron (needs O₂ + moisture)
  • LPG — mainly butane & propane; CNG — mainly methane

Elements & Alloys

  • Steel = Iron + Carbon | Stainless steel = Iron + Chromium + Nickel
  • Brass = Copper + Zinc | Bronze = Copper + Tin
  • Most abundant element in Earth's crust — Oxygen; in universe — Hydrogen
  • Noble gases: He, Ne, Ar, Kr, Xe, Rn — inert, don't react easily

Human body basics

  • Largest organ — Skin | Largest gland — Liver | Smallest bone — Stapes (ear)

  • Normal blood pressure — 120/80 mmHg | Blood pH — 7.35–7.45 (slightly alkaline)

  • Universal blood donor — O negative; universal recipient — AB positive
